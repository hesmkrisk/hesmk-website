A Risk-Based Approach

Security decisions should be driven by risk, not assumptions.

Security risk management provides leadership with a structured way to understand what matters, what could affect it, how exposed the organization is and which controls are proportionate to the risk.

Management Framework

Turning risk information into practical security action.

HESMK applies a structured management cycle so organizations can move from identifying security concerns to prioritizing treatment, assigning responsibility and strengthening resilience.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Establish Context

Understand organizational objectives, critical assets, operating environment, stakeholders and security priorities.

02 / IDENTIFY

Identify Risk

Identify relevant threats, vulnerabilities, exposure pathways and potential consequences to people, assets and operations.

03 / EVALUATE

Evaluate & Prioritize

Assess risk against organizational criteria and distinguish the issues requiring management attention first.

04 / TREAT

Develop Controls

Define proportionate security treatments that reduce exposure while considering operational requirements and cost.

05 / IMPLEMENT

Translate Into Action

Convert risk treatment decisions into practical measures, responsibilities, priorities and implementation plans.

06 / GOVERN

Monitor & Review

Maintain visibility of changing threats, vulnerabilities, controls, residual risk and management decisions.

07 / ASSURE

Close Security Gaps

Use structured gap analysis and verification to test whether existing security arrangements remain appropriate.

08 / RESILIENCE

Strengthen Continuity

Connect security risk treatment with crisis response, business continuity and organizational resilience.
