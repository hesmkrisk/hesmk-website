Our Approach

Security Risk Management — Not Assumptions

Traditional security approaches can apply the same controls regardless of the actual threat environment. HESMK takes a different approach to security risk management.

We help leadership understand what matters most, what could go wrong, where vulnerabilities exist and which security measures are proportionate to the organization's risk through structured security risk assessment and risk management.

Risk-Based Methodology

From Understanding Risk to Building Resilience

Security decisions should be grounded in evidence, proportionate to risk and designed to strengthen organizational resilience.

01

Understand

Establish context, critical assets, operating environment and risk appetite.

02

Identify

Identify credible threats, vulnerabilities, consequences and critical dependencies.

03

Assess

Analyze and evaluate security risks using structured, defensible methods.

04

Prioritize

Focus resources on risks that matter most to people, assets and operations.

05

Protect

Design proportionate physical, procedural and protective security controls.

06

Respond

Prepare leadership and teams to manage incidents, crises and disruption.

07

Resilience

Strengthen the organization's ability to absorb, adapt, recover and continue.
