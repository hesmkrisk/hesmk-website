THE PRINCIPLE

Counter-Terrorism Should Be Risk-Informed, Not Reactive

Effective counter-terrorism planning begins with understanding the threat environment, the assets that matter, the vulnerabilities that can be exploited and the consequences of disruption. HESMK connects threat intelligence and security assessment to practical protective decisions.

Threat Environment→Threat Analysis→Assets→Vulnerabilities→Consequences→Risk→Protective Measures

PROTECTIVE SECURITY PLANNING

From Threat Understanding to Protective Action

The objective is not to respond to every conceivable threat with the same level of security. HESMK focuses on proportionate measures that address credible exposure and reduce the opportunity for successful hostile action.

Threat & Vulnerability Assessment

Connect threat conditions with site, asset and organizational vulnerabilities to establish security priorities.

Protective Security Planning

Develop protective arrangements aligned with identified threats, vulnerabilities and consequences.

Security Risk Mapping

Translate assessed threats and vulnerabilities into a structured view of priority security risks.

Critical Infrastructure Protection

Support risk-based protection of critical facilities, systems and assets against hostile threats.

Event Security Risk Assessment

Assess threat exposure and security requirements for events, gatherings and other high-profile activities.

Vulnerability Reduction Planning

Prioritize practical measures to reduce exploitable weaknesses and strengthen protective security.
