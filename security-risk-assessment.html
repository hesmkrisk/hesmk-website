Why Assessment Matters

Security Measures Should Follow Risk — Not Assumptions

Security controls can become ineffective or unnecessarily costly when they are selected without a clear understanding of the threat environment, vulnerabilities, critical assets and potential consequences.

HESMK uses a risk-based approach to establish what needs protection, what could affect it, where exposure exists and which measures are proportionate to the organization's risk.

HESMK Assessment Approach

From Security Exposure to Risk-Informed Decisions

Our assessment process is designed to give leadership a practical picture of security exposure and a defensible basis for prioritizing treatment.

01 — ESTABLISH

Understand Context

Define the operating environment, objectives, locations, stakeholders and critical activities that shape the security requirement.

02 — IDENTIFY

Identify Assets & Threats

Identify people, property, information, operations and other critical assets alongside relevant hostile and non-hostile threats.

03 — ASSESS

Assess Vulnerability & Consequence

Examine weaknesses, exposure and potential consequences to determine where security risk is concentrated.

04 — PRIORITIZE

Evaluate & Prioritize Risk

Translate findings into prioritized risks that leadership can use to direct resources and security investment.

05 — TREAT

Develop Proportionate Controls

Identify practical security measures aligned with the assessed risk, operational requirements and available resources.

06 — PLAN

Risk Treatment Planning

Set priorities and actions for reducing exposure, improving protective measures and strengthening security effectiveness.

07 — VERIFY

Security Gap Analysis

Compare existing arrangements against the required level of protection and identify material gaps requiring attention.

08 — STRENGTHEN

Build Resilience

Connect security risk treatment with response, continuity and resilience so the organization can withstand and recover from disruption.
