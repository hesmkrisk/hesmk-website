THE PRINCIPLE

Security Controls Should Follow Risk — Not Assumptions

Effective physical security is not simply a collection of guards, gates, cameras and barriers. HESMK connects protective measures to the threat environment, vulnerabilities, asset criticality and consequences of loss.
