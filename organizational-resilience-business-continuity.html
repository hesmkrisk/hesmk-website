THE PRINCIPLE

Resilience Starts Before the Disruption

Organizational resilience is the ability to anticipate, withstand, respond to and recover from events that could disrupt critical operations. HESMK connects continuity planning with security risk, crisis management and operational priorities.

Risk-informed resilience logic

Context→Critical Operations→Disruption→Impact→Preparedness→Response→Recovery→Resilience
